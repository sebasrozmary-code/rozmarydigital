Frequently asked questions

How much budget do I need?

For local campaigns we recommend at least €300 per month. Less is possible, but reliable results take longer.

What is a sales funnel?

The path from first click to customer: ad, landing page, enquiry and follow-up. We build and measure every step.

Do you promise more revenue?

No. Nobody can honestly promise that. We do promise that every month you see what your ads bring in and what we adjust.

Why not just boost posts on Facebook?

Boosting mostly brings likes. A campaign with a goal, an audience and tracking brings enquiries.
