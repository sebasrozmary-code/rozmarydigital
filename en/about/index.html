One partner for everything digital

Rozmary Digital helps start-ups and small businesses win more customers, with websites, branding, marketing and the tools behind them.

Who we are

Behind Rozmary Digital is Sebastiaan, trained in business management and IT. He is your single point of contact: he listens to your business, safeguards the quality and follows up on everything. For photo, video and print we work with regular partners from the region.

How we use AI

We use smart AI tools for research, copy, design and code. That lets us deliver faster and at a better price. Every text, design and line of code is checked by a person before it goes live for you.

Where the name comes from

Rozmary refers to rosemary: a plant that grows all year round, is tough and stays green. It has long stood for remembrance. Exactly what a good partner does.

What you can expect from us

  • Prices agreed upfront
  • One point of contact, reachable on WhatsApp
  • Domain, copy and accounts in your name
  • No long contracts
  • Honest advice, even when that's ‘not needed yet’

Where we work

Our home base is the Kempen region in the province of Antwerp, where we're happy to visit you. Online we work across Flanders, Brussels and Wallonia, in English, Dutch and French.
