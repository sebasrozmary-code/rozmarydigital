Frequently asked questions

How much does a webshop cost?

With us, from €2,995 excl. VAT for a Shopify shop with 25 products. In Belgium, prices range from €1,250 to €25,000 or more, depending on custom work and integrations. On top of that you pay Shopify, from about €24 per month.

Why Shopify?

It's reliable and secure, and you manage it yourself without technical knowledge. For large catalogues or special integrations we build custom solutions.

Which rules apply to webshops in 2026?

Since 19 June 2026 you need a clear withdrawal button, and since 27 September 2026 new rules apply to warranty information and green claims. Our legal check lists everything for you.

Shopify or RozmaryPOS?

If you only sell online, Shopify is usually the simplest choice. If you also have a shop, RozmaryPOS is often better: the till and the webshop share one stock.

Can you connect my supplier?

Yes. For Thunder Racing we're building a connection with a supplier's catalogue, with automatic prices and stock.
