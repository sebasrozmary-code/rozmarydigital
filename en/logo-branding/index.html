Frequently asked questions

How much does a logo cost?

With us, €249 for Logo Start and €595 for a complete brand identity, excl. VAT. Online you'll see prices from €99 to €279 for just a logo.

Can ChatGPT make a logo?

AI tools quickly produce an image, but often without a unique character, without usable files and without clarity about the rights. We use AI for quick sketches and then design the logo ourselves.

Where can I make a logo for free?

With tools like Canva, and for a first test that's fine. If you want a logo that stays sharp at every size and suits your customers, a custom design pays off.

Who owns the rights?

After payment, all rights to the logo and brand identity are yours.
