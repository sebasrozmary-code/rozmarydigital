Marketing in five steps, without the jargon

Every service we sell belongs to one of these steps. So you always know why you're investing in something.

  1. 1

    Vision

    Who are you the best choice for?

    We define your audience, your offer and your prices. So everyone knows why they should choose you.

  2. 2

    Visibility

    Are you found where your customers search?

    Google, your Google profile, advertising and social media: we make you visible at the moment someone searches.

  3. 3

    Trust

    Why should they choose you?

    A site, reviews, real photos and examples that show why they should pick you over the competition.

  4. 4

    Conversion

    Does a visitor become a customer?

    WhatsApp, quote, appointment or online payment, with automatic follow-up. No enquiry gets lost.

  5. 5

    Retention

    Does the customer come back and bring others?

    Asking for reviews, a newsletter and maintenance. A loyal customer costs less than a new one.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website from Rozmary cost?

A one-page website costs €995 excl. VAT, a site with 8 to 15 pages €2,495. Rather not pay a deposit? Choose the Starter Package at €99 a month.

Can't ChatGPT just build my website?

AI can put together a first version, and we use it too. The difference is what comes next: the right keywords, fast technology, accurate copy, tracking and someone who steps in when something goes wrong.

How fast is my site online?

A one-page site goes live within 7 working days after we receive your logo, copy and photos. If we miss that, you get €100 off.

Am I tied to a long contract?

Not for websites and monthly plans: minimum 3 months, then cancel monthly. Only RozmaryPOS runs for 36 months, because installation and integrations are included. Your domain and your site always remain yours.

Do you work outside the Kempen region?

Yes. We work across Flanders, Brussels and Wallonia, in English, Dutch or French. In the Kempen region we're happy to visit you.

Curious where your business is losing customers?

Ask for a free growth scan. We review your website, Google profile, reviews and three competitors, and talk it through in 15 minutes.
