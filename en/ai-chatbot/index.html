Frequently asked questions

Is a chatbot legally compliant?

Yes, as long as it says it's an AI. That has been mandatory under the European AI Act since 2 August 2026, and our chatbots do it by default.

What if the bot can't answer a question?

It says so honestly and asks for your customer's contact details, so you can follow up.

What data does the bot use?

Only what you provide: your website, prices and FAQ. Conversations are stored according to your privacy policy.

Can the bot book appointments?

Yes, with a connection to your online calendar.
