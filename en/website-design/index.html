Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

With us, €995 for a one-page site and €2,495 for a site with 8 to 15 pages, excl. VAT. In Belgium, prices range from €550 with a freelancer to €7,000 or more with larger agencies.

Can I pay for a website monthly?

Yes. With the Starter Package you pay €99 per month for 24 months, with no deposit. Hosting and maintenance are included.

Can ChatGPT build a website?

It can produce a rough version. What's missing is the rest: keyword research, fast hosting, correct Google data, tracking and someone to maintain your site. That's what we do, and we use AI to make it faster and more affordable.

Who owns the website when it's finished?

You do. The domain is registered in your name and you get all the files. If you stop our maintenance, we help you move.
